Lesson 2

explanation

Worked examples, one shape

We now work the compound expression through to the end.

Example 2.1checked

Evaluate \frac{-15 \div 3 - (-2) \times (-4) + 6}{-2 \times 3 + (-4)}

  1. -15 \div 3 = -5Division comes before addition, so the numerator starts here.
  2. -(-2) \times (-4) = -8The product (-2)\times(-4) is 8, and the minus in front of it makes the term -8.
  3. -5 - 8 + 6 = -7Carrying the sign through, the numerator is -7.
  4. -2 \times 3 + (-4) = -10The denominator, worked the same way.
  5. \frac{-7}{-10} = \frac{7}{10}A negative divided by a negative is positive.

Answer\frac{7}{10}

Example 2.2checked

Evaluate \frac{-15 \div 3 - (-2)(-4) + 6}{-2 \times 3 + (-4)} — written with implicit products

  1. (-2)(-4) = 8Brackets written side by side mean multiply.
  2. \frac{-7}{-10} = \frac{7}{10}The same expression, the same value.

Answer\frac{7}{10}
